## 💸 **Hidden Costs Buyers Don't Think About**

### **When Hiring a Developer, Buyers Also Pay For:**

#### **1. Finding the Developer:**
- Time spent searching
- Interviewing candidates
- Checking portfolios
- **Cost:** 1-2 weeks of time

#### **2. Communication & Management:**
- Explaining requirements
- Reviewing progress
- Giving feedback
- Managing changes
- **Cost:** 20-30% of development time

#### **3. Testing & Bug Fixes:**
- Finding bugs
- Reporting issues
- Waiting for fixes
- Re-testing
- **Cost:** 20-30% of development time

#### **4. UI/UX Refinements:**
- Design changes
- User feedback
- Iterations
- **Cost:** 10-20% of development time

#### **5. Risk Factors:**
- Developer might disappear
- Developer might deliver late
- Developer might deliver poor quality
- Developer might not understand requirements
- **Cost:** High risk, potential project failure

#### **6. Opportunity Cost:**
- Time spent managing developer
- Time not spent on business
- **Cost:** Lost business opportunities

**Total Hidden Costs: 50-80% of development time**

**Example:**
- Developer says: "3 days, 450,000 RWF"
- Hidden costs: +50% = **4.5 days + 675,000 RWF**
- **Real cost: 4.5 days + 675,000 RWF**
