# 🎨 Create Feature Graphic on Canva - Step by Step

## 📐 **REQUIREMENTS:**
- **Size:** 1024 x 500 pixels
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **Max size:** 15 MB

---

## 🚀 **STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE:**

### **STEP 1: Go to Canva**

1. **Visit:** https://www.canva.com
2. **Sign in** (or create free account if needed)
3. **Click:** "Create a design" (top right)

---

### **STEP 2: Create Custom Size**

1. **Click:** "Custom size" (at the bottom of the template list)
2. **Enter dimensions:**
   - **Width:** 1024
   - **Height:** 500
   - **Unit:** Pixels (px)
3. **Click:** "Create new design"

---

### **STEP 3: Design Your Feature Graphic**

### **Option A: Simple & Professional (Recommended)**

**Layout:**
- **Left side (40%):** Large app icon
- **Right side (60%):** App name + tagline

**Steps:**

1. **Add Background:**
   - Click "Background" (left sidebar)
   - Choose a gradient or solid color
   - **Suggestion:** Teal to green gradient (matching your app theme)
   - OR: Solid teal background

2. **Add Your App Icon:**
   - Click "Uploads" (left sidebar)
   - Click "Upload media"
   - Upload your `FinWise_Icon.png`
   - Drag it to the left side
   - Resize to about 300-350 pixels wide
   - Center it vertically

3. **Add App Name:**
   - Click "Text" (left sidebar)
   - Click "Add a heading"
   - Type: **"FinWise"**
   - **Font:** Bold, Sans-serif (like Montserrat Bold, or Poppins Bold)
   - **Size:** 80-100px
   - **Color:** White
   - Position on right side, top area

4. **Add Tagline:**
   - Click "Text" again
   - Click "Add a subheading"
   - Type: **"Smart Personal Finance"** or **"Track. Budget. Achieve."**
   - **Font:** Regular, Sans-serif
   - **Size:** 40-50px
   - **Color:** White or light yellow
   - Position below app name

5. **Add Feature Points (Optional):**
   - Click "Text"
   - Add bullet points:
     * "Track Expenses"
     * "Manage Budgets"
     * "Achieve Goals"
   - **Font:** Regular
   - **Size:** 24-30px
   - **Color:** White (semi-transparent)

---

### **Option B: With Visual Elements**

**Add icons or graphics:**

1. **Search for icons:**
   - Click "Elements" (left sidebar)
   - Search: "money", "wallet", "chart", "shield"
   - Add 2-3 small icons
   - Position around the text

2. **Add shapes:**
   - Click "Elements" → "Shapes"
   - Add circles or rectangles as accents
   - Make them semi-transparent

---

### **STEP 4: Final Touches**

1. **Check alignment:**
   - Make sure everything is centered
   - Use alignment tools (top toolbar)

2. **Add subtle effects (optional):**
   - Select elements
   - Add slight shadow or glow
   - Keep it subtle!

3. **Preview:**
   - Zoom out to see full design
   - Make sure text is readable
   - Check that it looks professional

---

### **STEP 5: Download**

1. **Click:** "Download" (top right)
2. **Select:** "PNG" or "JPG"
3. **Quality:** High or Best
4. **Click:** "Download"
5. **Save as:** `FinWise_Feature_Graphic.png`

---

## 🎨 **DESIGN TIPS:**

### **Colors to Use:**
- **Background:** Teal gradient (#00BFA5 to #00E676) or solid teal
- **Text:** White (for contrast)
- **Accents:** Yellow (#FFD600) or light green

### **Font Suggestions:**
- **App Name:** Montserrat Bold, Poppins Bold, or Inter Bold
- **Tagline:** Montserrat Regular, Poppins Regular

### **Layout Ideas:**

**Layout 1: Icon Left, Text Right**
```
[Icon]  FinWise
        Smart Personal Finance
        Track • Budget • Achieve
```

**Layout 2: Centered**
```
        FinWise
        Smart Personal Finance
    [Icon in center]
```

**Layout 3: Icon Right**
```
FinWise              [Icon]
Smart Personal Finance
Track • Budget • Achieve
```

## 💡 **CANVA SHORTCUTS:**

- **Duplicate:** Ctrl+D (Windows) or Cmd+D (Mac)
- **Align:** Select elements → Use alignment tools
- **Group:** Select multiple → Right-click → Group
- **Undo:** Ctrl+Z (Windows) or Cmd+Z (Mac)

---

## 🎯 **AFTER CREATING:**

1. **Download** your feature graphic
2. **Check file size** (should be under 15 MB)
3. **Upload to Play Console:**
   - Go to: Store presence → Main store listing
   - Find: "Feature graphic"
   - Click: "Upload"
   - Select your file
   - Save
