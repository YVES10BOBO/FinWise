FINWISE PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 2026

Welcome to FinWise! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By using FinWise, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email Address: Used for account authentication and communication
- Name: Used to personalize your experience within the app
- Password: Securely hashed and stored by Firebase Authentication (we never see your actual password)

1.2 Financial Data
To provide our financial tracking services, we collect:
- Transactions: Income and expense entries including amount, category, date, description, and transaction type
- Income Information: Your usual income amount and frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly)
- Spending Categories: Custom spending categories you create and use
- Financial Goals: Savings goals including target amount, current progress, and deadlines
- Spending Patterns: Information about your spending style and preferences

1.3 Profile Data
Optional information you may provide:
- Profile Picture: If you choose to upload a profile photo, it is stored securely

1.4 Device Data
For offline functionality and improved performance:
- Local Cache: Your data is cached locally on your device for offline access
- App Preferences: Settings such as dark mode preference

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide Core Services: Enable financial tracking, budgeting, and goal management features
- Sync Across Devices: Synchronize your data across multiple devices when you log in with the same account
- Generate Insights: Calculate financial health scores, spending analytics, and budget recommendations
- Personalize Experience: Customize the app interface with your name and preferences
- Improve the App: Analyze usage patterns to enhance functionality and fix bugs
- Maintain Security: Protect your account and data from unauthorized access

3. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

3.1 Cloud Storage
Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform), which provides:
- Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (using TLS/SSL) and at rest
- Secure Authentication: Firebase Authentication ensures only you can access your account
- Access Controls: Firestore security rules ensure each user can only access their own data
- Data Isolation: Your data is stored in separate, private collections tied to your user ID

3.2 Local Storage
For offline functionality, your data is also cached locally on your device using secure local storage mechanisms.

3.3 Security Measures
- We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information
- Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are securely hashed by Firebase
- All API communications are encrypted
- We regularly review and update our security practices

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

4.1 Firebase Services
FinWise uses the following Firebase (Google) services:
- Firebase Authentication: For secure user authentication
- Cloud Firestore: For storing your financial data and profile information
- Firebase Storage: For storing profile pictures

These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.2 Data Sharing
We do NOT:
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your financial data with advertisers
- Use your data for marketing purposes without your consent
- Share your information with other users

5. YOUR RIGHTS & CONTROL

You have full control over your data:

5.1 Access
- View all your transactions, goals, and profile information within the app
- Access your data anytime by logging into your account

5.2 Export
- Export your transactions as CSV files
- Generate PDF reports of your financial data
- Download your data for backup or migration purposes

5.3 Modification
- Edit or delete individual transactions
- Update your profile information, income, and categories
- Modify or delete financial goals
- Change or remove your profile picture

5.4 Deletion
- Delete individual transactions or goals
- Clear all transactions or goals at once
- Logout to stop cloud synchronization (local cached data will be cleared)
- Delete your account by contacting us (all associated data will be permanently removed)

6. DATA RETENTION

- Your data is retained for as long as your account is active
- When you delete your account, all associated data (transactions, goals, profile information, profile pictures) is permanently deleted from our servers
- Local cached data is cleared when you logout
- We do not retain your data after account deletion

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

FinWise is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Your data may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country of residence. Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) operates globally, and your data may be transferred to and stored in the United States or other countries. By using FinWise, you consent to the transfer of your data to these locations.

9. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
- Posting a notice in the app (for significant changes)

Your continued use of FinWise after any changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. YOUR CONSENT

By using FinWise, you consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our app.

11. CONTACT US

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:
- Through the app: Settings → About → Help & Support

We will respond to your inquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
