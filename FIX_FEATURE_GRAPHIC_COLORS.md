# 🎨 Fix Feature Graphic Colors - Alternative Solution

## 🎯 **PROBLEM:**
- Background is light green (can't change gradient - not free)
- White text doesn't show well on light background
- Need better contrast

## ✅ **SOLUTION:**
Change text colors to **dark colors** that contrast with light background!

---

## 🎨 **RECOMMENDED COLOR CHANGES:**

### **Option 1: Dark Teal/Blue (Best Contrast)**

**"FinWise" (App Name):**
- Color: **Dark teal** or **Dark blue**
- Hex code: `#00695C` or `#004D40` or `#0277BD`
- This will stand out on light green background

**"Smart Personal Finance" (Tagline):**
- Color: **Dark green** or **Dark teal**
- Hex code: `#2E7D32` or `#00695C`
- Slightly lighter than app name, but still dark

**Features List:**
- Color: **Dark gray** or **Dark green**
- Hex code: `#424242` or `#2E7D32`

---

### **Option 2: Black/Dark Gray (Simple & Clear)**

**"FinWise" (App Name):**
- Color: **Black** or **Very dark gray**
- Hex code: `#000000` or `#212121`
- Maximum contrast

**"Smart Personal Finance" (Tagline):**
- Color: **Dark gray**
- Hex code: `#424242` or `#616161`

**Features List:**
- Color: **Dark gray**
- Hex code: `#616161`

---

### **Option 3: Dark Blue (Professional)**

**"FinWise" (App Name):**
- Color: **Dark blue**
- Hex code: `#1565C0` or `#0D47A1`

**"Smart Personal Finance" (Tagline):**
- Color: **Medium blue**
- Hex code: `#1976D2` or `#1565C0`

**Features List:**
- Color: **Dark gray**
- Hex code: `#424242`

---

## 🛠️ **HOW TO CHANGE IN CANVA:**

### **Step 1: Change "FinWise" Color**

1. **Click** on "FinWise" text
2. **Click** the color picker (text color icon)
3. **Choose one:**
   - Click "Custom color" or color wheel
   - Enter hex code: `#00695C` (dark teal)
   - OR: `#000000` (black)
   - OR: `#1565C0` (dark blue)
4. **Apply** the color

### **Step 2: Change "Smart Personal Finance" Color**

1. **Click** on "Smart Personal Finance" text
2. **Click** the color picker
3. **Choose one:**
   - Hex code: `#2E7D32` (dark green)
   - OR: `#424242` (dark gray)
   - OR: `#1976D2` (medium blue)
4. **Apply** the color

### **Step 3: Change Features List Color**

1. **Click** on the features list text
2. **Click** the color picker
3. **Choose:**
   - Hex code: `#424242` (dark gray)
   - OR: `#616161` (medium gray)
4. **Apply** the color

## 💡 **TIP:**

If Canva doesn't accept hex codes:
1. Click the color picker
2. Use the color wheel
3. Drag to **dark teal/blue/green** area
4. Or select from "Dark" color palette
