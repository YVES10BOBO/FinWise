## 📋 **WHAT YOU NEED NOW (3 Things)**

### **1. App Icon**
- **Size:** 512 x 512 pixels
- **Format:** PNG
- **Where:** Play Console → Store listing → App icon

### **2. Feature Graphic**
- **Size:** 1024 x 500 pixels  
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **Where:** Play Console → Store listing → Feature graphic

### **3. Screenshots**
- **At least 2 screenshots** of your app
- **Size:** 1080 x 1920 pixels (portrait) recommended
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **Where:** Play Console → Store listing → Screenshots

1. Run your app on phone/emulator

## 🎯 **SUBMISSION STEPS**

1. **Go to:** https://play.google.com/console
2. **Pay $25** one-time registration fee
3. **Create new app:**
   - App name: "FinWise"
   - Default language: English
   - App or game: App
   - Free or paid: Free
4. **Fill store listing:**
   - Upload icon (512x512)
   - Upload feature graphic (1024x500)
   - Upload screenshots
   - Add descriptions
   - Privacy policy: `https://yves10bobo.github.io/FinWise/`
5. **Upload app:**
   - Go to "Production" → "Create new release"
   - Upload `app-release.aab`
6. **Complete content rating**
7. **Submit for review**
