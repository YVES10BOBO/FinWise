# 🧪 Closed Testing Requirements - Before Production

## ✅ **THIS IS NORMAL - NOT A MISTAKE!**

Google Play **requires** you to complete closed testing before you can publish to production. This is mandatory for all new apps.

---

## 📋 **WHAT YOU NEED TO DO:**

### **STEP 1: Go to Closed Testing**

1. **In Play Console**, go to: **Testing** → **Closed testing**
2. **Click:** "Create new release" or "Create new track"

---

### **STEP 2: Upload Your App Bundle**

1. **You need:** Your app bundle file
   - Location: `build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`
   - If you don't have it, build it first

2. **Upload:**
   - Click "Create new release"
   - Upload your `.aab` file
   - Add release notes (e.g., "Initial release")
   - Save

---

### **STEP 3: Get Testers**

**You need at least 12 testers** who will:
- Opt-in to your closed test
- Test your app
- Provide feedback (optional)

**How to add testers:**

1. **Go to:** Testing → Closed testing → Testers
2. **Click:** "Create email list" or "Add testers"
3. **Add emails:**
   - Your own email
   - Friends, family, colleagues
   - At least 12 email addresses
4. **Save** the list

---

### **STEP 4: Share Testing Link**

1. **Get the testing link:**
   - Go to: Testing → Closed testing
   - Copy the "Opt-in URL" or testing link
2. **Share with testers:**
   - Send them the link
   - They click it and opt-in
   - They can then download and test your app

---

### **STEP 5: Run Test for 14 Days**

**Requirements:**
- ✅ At least 12 testers must opt-in
- ✅ Test must run for at least 14 days
- ✅ Testers should actually use the app (optional but recommended)

**After 14 days:**
- You can apply for production access
- Go to Dashboard → Apply for production

---

## 🚀 **QUICK STEPS:**

1. **Go to:** Testing → Closed testing
2. **Create new release**
3. **Upload:** `app-release.aab`
4. **Add testers:** At least 12 emails
5. **Share testing link** with testers
6. **Wait 14 days**
7. **Apply for production**
