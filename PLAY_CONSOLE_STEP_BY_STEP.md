# 📱 Play Console - Step by Step Guide

## 🎯 **CURRENT PAGE: "Create App" Form**

Fill out each section below:

---

## **1. APP NAME** ✅

**Field:** "App name"
**What to enter:** `FinWise`
- This is how your app appears on Google Play
- Max 30 characters
- You can change this later if needed

**Enter:** `FinWise` (or `FinWise Tracker` if you prefer)

---

## **2. DEFAULT LANGUAGE** ✅

**Field:** "Default language"
**Status:** Already set to `English (United States) – en-US`
**Action:** ✅ No action needed - this is correct!

---

## **3. APP OR GAME** ✅

**Field:** "App or game"
**What to select:** Click **"App"** (not "Game")
- Your app is a finance application, so select "App"
- You can change this later if needed

**Action:** Click the **"App"** button/radio button

---

## **4. FREE OR PAID** ✅

**Field:** "Free or paid"
**What to select:** Click **"Free"**
- Your app is free to download
- You can change this later (before publishing)
- Once published as free, you cannot change to paid

**Action:** Click the **"Free"** button/radio button

---

## **5. DECLARATIONS** ✅

You need to check **3 boxes**:

### **Box 1: Developer Program Policies**
**Text:** "Confirm app meets the Developer Program Policies"
**Action:** ✅ **Check this box**
- This confirms your app follows Google's rules
- Your app is compliant (no inappropriate content, proper privacy policy, etc.)

### **Box 2: Play App Signing**
**Text:** "Accept the Play App Signing Terms of Service"
**Action:** ✅ **Check this box**
- Required to publish Android App Bundles (.aab files)
- Google manages your app signing key
- This is standard and safe

### **Box 3: US Export Laws**
**Text:** "Accept US export laws"
**Action:** ✅ **Check this box**
- Standard requirement for all apps
- Your app uses standard encryption (Firebase)
- No special export restrictions needed

---

## ✅ **COMPLETE FORM SUMMARY**

Fill out like this:

```
App name: FinWise
Default language: English (United States) – en-US ✅ (already set)
App or game: App ✅
Free or paid: Free ✅
Declarations:
  ☑ Confirm app meets the Developer Program Policies
  ☑ Accept the Play App Signing Terms of Service
  ☑ Accept US export laws
```

---

## 🚀 **AFTER FILLING THE FORM**

1. **Click "Create app"** button (usually at bottom of page)
2. **Wait for app to be created** (takes a few seconds)
3. **You'll be redirected** to your app's dashboard

---

## 📋 **WHAT HAPPENS NEXT**

After creating the app, you'll see:
- App dashboard
- Store presence section (where you add icon, screenshots, descriptions)
- Production section (where you upload your app bundle)
- Policy section (where you complete content rating)

---

## ⚠️ **IMPORTANT NOTES**

- **App name:** You can change this later in Store settings
- **Free/Paid:** Can change before publishing, but not after
- **Declarations:** All 3 must be checked to proceed
- **No mistakes:** Don't worry - you can edit most things later!

---
