## 🎨 **REQUIRED ASSETS FOR PLAY STORE**

### **1. APP ICON** (Required)

**Size:** `512 x 512 pixels` (PNG, 32-bit)
- **Format:** PNG (no transparency)
- **File size:** Max 1 MB
- **Design rules:**
  - No rounded corners (Google adds them automatically)
  - No shadows or effects
  - Icon should be centered
  - Use solid background color
  - Text should be minimal and readable at small sizes

**Where to upload:** Play Console → Store presence → Main store listing → App icon

---

### **2. FEATURE GRAPHIC** (Required)

**Size:** `1024 x 500 pixels` (PNG or JPG)
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **File size:** Max 15 MB
- **Design tips:**
  - This appears at the top of your Play Store listing
  - Include your app name "FinWise"
  - Show key features visually
  - Use your brand colors
  - Keep text minimal (it's a banner)

**Where to upload:** Play Console → Store presence → Main store listing → Feature graphic

---

### **3. SCREENSHOTS** (Required - At least 2)

**Requirements:**
- **Phone screenshots:** At least 2, up to 8
- **Size:** 
  - Minimum: `320 x 320 pixels`
  - Maximum: `3840 x 3840 pixels`
  - Recommended: `1080 x 1920 pixels` (portrait) or `1920 x 1080 pixels` (landscape)
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 or 9:16 (portrait recommended for mobile apps)
- **File size:** Max 8 MB each

**What to show:**
1. Home/Dashboard screen (showing financial overview)
2. Transaction list/Add transaction
3. Goals screen
4. Budget/Insights screen
5. Settings/Profile screen

**Where to upload:** Play Console → Store presence → Main store listing → Screenshots

## 🎨 **QUICK ICON DESIGN TIPS**

### **App Icon (512x512):**
1. **Use your brand colors** (FinWise theme)
2. **Include a simple logo or symbol** (money, wallet, chart icon)
3. **Make it recognizable** at small sizes
4. **Avoid too much detail**
5. **Test how it looks** at 48x48 pixels (actual size on phone)

### **Feature Graphic (1024x500):**
1. **Left side:** App icon or logo (large)
2. **Right side:** Key features or tagline
3. **Background:** Use gradient or solid color matching your app theme
4. **Text:** "FinWise - Smart Personal Finance" or similar
