# 📱 Play Console Dashboard - Step by Step Guide

## 🎯 **YOU ARE HERE: Dashboard**

You need to complete these steps **IN ORDER**:

---

## ✅ **STEP 1: FINISH SETTING UP YOUR APP** (Do This First!)

Click on **"Finish setting up your app"** section.

### **Part A: Let us know about the content of your app**

You'll need to complete these sections:

#### **1. Set privacy policy** ✅
- **Click:** "Set privacy policy"
- **Enter:** `https://yves10bobo.github.io/FinWise/`
- **Save**

#### **2. App access** ✅
- **Click:** "App access"
- **Select:** "All or some functionality is restricted" (if your app requires login)
- OR **Select:** "All functionality is available without restriction" (if no login required)
- **Note:** Since FinWise uses Firebase Auth, select "All or some functionality is restricted"
- **Save**

#### **3. Ads** ✅
- **Click:** "Ads"
- **Select:** "No, my app does not contain ads"
- **Save**

#### **4. Content rating** ✅
- **Click:** "Content rating"
- **Complete questionnaire:**
  - Does your app contain user-generated content? → **No**
  - Does your app allow users to interact? → **No** (or Yes if you have social features)
  - Does your app contain violence, sexual content, etc.? → **No** to all
  - Does your app contain gambling? → **No**
  - Does your app allow in-app purchases? → **No** (or Yes if you plan to add premium features)
- **Result:** Should be rated "Everyone" or "For everyone"
- **Save**

#### **5. Target audience** ✅
- **Click:** "Target audience"
- **Select:** "13+ years" or "For everyone"
- **Designed for families:** Yes (optional)
- **Save**

#### **6. Data safety** ✅
- **Click:** "Data safety"
- **This is important!** Fill out:
  - **Data collected:** Yes
  - **Personal info:** Email address, Name (optional)
  - **Financial info:** Yes (transactions, budgets, goals)
  - **App activity:** Usage data
  - **Data sharing:** With Firebase/Google (for app functionality)
  - **Data encryption:** Yes (in transit and at rest)
  - **Data deletion:** Users can request deletion
- **Save**

#### **7. Government apps** ✅
- **Click:** "Government apps"
- **Select:** "No, this is not a government app"
- **Save**

#### **8. Financial features** ✅
- **Click:** "Financial features"
- **Select:** "Yes, my app handles financial transactions"
- **Type:** Personal finance management
- **Details:** Expense tracking, budget management, financial goals
- **Save**

#### **9. Health** ✅
- **Click:** "Health"
- **Select:** "No, my app does not handle health data"
- **Save**

#### **1. Select an app category and provide contact details** ✅
- **Click:** "Select an app category and provide contact details"
- **Category:** Select **"Finance"**
- **Contact details:**
  - **Email:** `yvesrutembeza@gmail.com`
  - **Phone:** (optional, leave blank if you don't have one)

#### **2. Set up your store listing** ✅ **IMPORTANT!**
- **Click:** "Set up your store listing"
- **This is where you add:**
  - App icon (512x512)
  - Feature graphic (1024x500)
  - Screenshots (2-8 images)
  - Short description (80 chars)
  - Full description (4000 chars)
- **Complete all required fields**
- **Save**

## ✅ **STEP 2: CLOSED TESTING** (After Step 1)

Once you've finished setting up your app:

1. **Click:** "Closed testing"
2. **Create a test track:**
   - Name: "Internal Test" or "Beta Test"
   - Add testers (you can add your own email)
3. **Upload your app bundle:**
   - Go to "Releases" → "Create new release"
   - Upload: `app-release.aab`
   - Add release notes
   - Save
4. **Get testers:**
   - You need at least 12 testers (can be friends, family, or use a testing service)
   - Share the testing link with them
   - They need to opt-in
5. **Run test for 14 days:**
   - Keep the test running for at least 14 days
   - Get feedback and fix any issues

---

## ✅ **STEP 3: PRODUCTION** (Final Step)

After closed testing (14 days, 12+ testers):

1. **Click:** "Production"
2. **Apply for production access**
3. **Answer questions about your closed test**
4. **Upload production release**
5. **Submit for review**
6. **Wait 1-7 days for approval**
7. **App goes live!** 🎉
