### **13. What problem does your project solve?**

**Option 1: Comprehensive Version**

```
FinWise solves multiple interconnected problems related to personal finance management in Rwanda:

(1) Lack of Financial Visibility: Many people do not know where their money goes. They spend without tracking, making it impossible to identify wasteful spending or understand spending patterns. FinWise provides easy expense tracking and visual analytics so users can see exactly where their money goes.

(2) Difficulty Maintaining Budgets: Without a structured system, people struggle to set and stick to budgets. FinWise offers intelligent budget recommendations based on income and spending patterns, with visual progress indicators that help users stay on track and avoid overspending.

(3) Challenges in Saving Money: Without clear goals or progress tracking, saving feels overwhelming. FinWise enables users to set specific financial goals (e.g., save for tuition, a phone, or emergency fund) and track progress with motivating visual indicators.

(4) Financial Literacy Gap: Many individuals lack access to tools that teach good money habits. FinWise serves as a practical, hands-on tool that builds financial literacy through daily use—helping users understand income vs. expenses, savings rates, and healthy spending habits.

(5) Fragmented Solutions: People often use notebooks, spreadsheets, or multiple apps that don't work together. FinWise provides an all-in-one solution—tracking, budgeting, goals, and analytics—in a single, user-friendly mobile app designed for Rwanda (RWF, Mobile Money support).
```
