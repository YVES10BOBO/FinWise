# FinWise - App Summary for Feedback

## 🎯 **What is FinWise?**

FinWise is a personal finance management app designed for people in Rwanda to track their money, manage budgets, and achieve financial goals - all in one simple app.

---

## ❌ **Problems People Face:**

### **Problem 1: "I don't know where my money goes"**
- People spend money but can't remember what they bought
- No way to track daily expenses
- Hard to see spending patterns

### **Problem 2: "I can't stick to my budget"**
- No easy way to set budgets for different categories
- Can't see if they're overspending until it's too late
- No visual feedback on spending

### **Problem 3: "I can't save money"**
- No clear savings goals
- Can't track progress toward goals
- Don't know how much they can save

### **Problem 4: "I don't understand my finances"**
- Can't see overall financial health
- No clear picture of income vs expenses
- Don't know if they're spending too much

### **Problem 5: "Managing money is complicated"**
- Need multiple apps or notebooks
- Hard to organize transactions
- No single place to see everything

---

## ✅ **How FinWise Solves These Problems:**

### **Solution 1: Easy Expense Tracking**
**Feature:** Add transactions quickly
- Tap "+" button to add income or expense
- Choose category (Food, Transport, Rent, etc.)
- Select account type (Cash, Bank, Mobile Money)
- See all transactions in one place

**Why it helps:**
- Never forget where money went
- Track every expense in seconds
- See complete spending history

---

### **Solution 2: Smart Budget Management**
**Feature:** Category budgets with visual tracking
- 20+ spending categories (Food, Transport, Entertainment, etc.)
- Budget recommendations for each category
- Visual progress bars showing spending vs budget
- Pie charts showing where money goes

**Why it helps:**
- Set budgets for each category
- See if you're overspending before it's too late
- Understand spending patterns visually

---

### **Solution 3: Financial Goals Tracking**
**Feature:** Set and track savings goals
- Create goals (e.g., "Save 100,000 RWF for vacation")
- Track progress with visual indicators
- Contribute money to goals manually
- See how close you are to achieving goals

**Why it helps:**
- Stay motivated to save
- See progress toward goals
- Plan for big purchases

---

### **Solution 4: Financial Health Dashboard**
**Feature:** Complete financial overview
- See total balance at a glance
- Income and expense statistics
- Savings rate calculation
- Financial health score (0-100)
- Personalized greeting with your name

**Why it helps:**
- Understand overall financial situation
- See if you're saving or spending too much
- Get a health score to improve

---

### **Solution 5: All-in-One Solution**
**Feature:** Everything in one app
- Track transactions
- Manage budgets
- Set goals
- View analytics
- Calendar view
- Export to PDF/CSV
- Dark mode

**Why it helps:**
- No need for multiple apps
- Everything organized in one place
- Easy to use and understand

## 💡 **How to Use FinWise:**

1. **Sign Up** - Create account with email
2. **Onboarding** - Set your income and spending categories (2 minutes)
3. **Add Transactions** - Start tracking income and expenses
4. **Set Budgets** - Use budget recommendations or set your own
5. **Create Goals** - Set savings goals and track progress
6. **Monitor** - Check dashboard regularly to see your financial health
