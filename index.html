What FinWise does

FinWise is a personal finance tracker built for Mobile Money users. It reads Mobile Money and bank SMS alerts on your device and records your transactions automatically, so your balance stays accurate without manual entry.

Features

  • Automatic transaction detection from Mobile Money and bank SMS
  • Savings goals — reserve money towards what you're saving for
  • Spending insights — see where your money goes by category
  • Cash flow analysis — money in vs money out, month by month
  • App lock — PIN and fingerprint protection
  • 13 currencies supported
  • Works offline — syncs when you reconnect

Your privacy

SMS message content is processed entirely on your phone and is never uploaded. Your PIN is stored only as an irreversible hash, and fingerprint data never reaches the app. We show no advertising and never sell your data.
